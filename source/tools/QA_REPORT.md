# QA report: Pip & Python, chapters 1–10 and home page

Independent review, 2026-11-07. No site files were edited.

**How it was checked**
- Read AUTHORING.md.
- Ran `tools/check_chapter.js` on every chapter. All 20 runs PASS, with zero console errors.
- Looked at the full-page screenshots as contact sheets at 1280 px and 390 px.
- Drove the pages with Playwright for deeper states:
  - steppers and maps part-way through
  - every `<details>` opened one at a time at 390 px
  - wrong answers in predict, trace and spot exercises
  - a passed check, followed by a reload of the home page on an emulated iPhone 13
- Ran DOM scans for:
  - text that is clipped or overflows
  - text whose rendered size is below 11 px
  - one-sided borders
  - text contrast below 7:1
  - the character and data attributes used on each page
- Re-ran version claims on CPython 3.15.2 and 3.13.18.
- Read the main-path text of every chapter, plus the recaps and the predict and trace explanations against the real captured output.

**Checked and found fine**
- Version-difference claims all reproduce on the two interpreters: ch1 "Did you mean 'in'?", ch3 ZeroDivisionError wording, ch6 kwarg hint, ch7 unpack count and tuple hint, ch8 unhashable wording, ch9 AttributeError hint and `except A, B`, ch10 UTF-8 default.
- Every explanation I sampled (around 60 predict and trace keys) matches the captured output.
- No page still mentions 3.15.0b4.
- The score saves to localStorage and shows on the home shelf.
- Top-nav labels and order are identical in all 10 chapters, and `aria-current` is correct.
- Each chapter's "next" link points to the right file.
- No thick one-sided accent-stripe borders anywhere. The only one-sided borders are the 5 px underline on `.anat-line .part` and the 3 px input underline.
- Text contrast is at least 7:1 everywhere except the one ch4 case listed below.
