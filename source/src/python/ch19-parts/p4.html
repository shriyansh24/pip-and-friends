Dogs, cats and fish are all pets. They share a lot (a name, a description) and differ a little (the sound they make). Copying the Pet class three times would be silly.

Picture: the Pet cutter at the top of a family tree, with child cutters Dog, Cat and Fish below it.

Pip says: So we make a family tree. Dog is a subclass (a child) of Pet, its parent or base class. A child gets everything the parent has, for free. That's inheritance. Read class Dog(Pet) as "a Dog is a Pet".
